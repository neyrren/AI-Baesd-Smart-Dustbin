The Problem

Millions of tonnes of waste are mismanaged every year

Mixed waste in landfills produces methane — a greenhouse gas 80 times more potent than CO₂. Proper sorting is the first step to reversing this.

Organic Waste

Food scraps and garden waste deserve better than landfill

Organic waste composted correctly returns nutrients to the soil. When mixed with plastics, that opportunity is lost forever.

Inorganic Waste

Plastics and metals are resources — not rubbish

Recyclable materials recovered from inorganic waste reduce the demand for raw material extraction, saving energy and protecting natural environments.

Our Solution

AI that sorts waste automatically — no human effort required

This system classifies every item deposited into the bin using deep learning and computer vision — no human effort required.

Sorting waste is one of the most impactful things an institution can do

Universities, hospitals, and offices generate enormous quantities of waste daily. When that waste is not segregated — when banana peels end up with plastic bottles and paper cups — it all goes to landfill, contaminating potentially recyclable materials and accelerating environmental degradation.

This AI-based smart dustbin eliminates the need to rely on people making the right sorting decision. The moment waste is placed in front of the bin, the system's camera captures it, the AI classifies it, and the servo motor directs it to the correct compartment — organic or inorganic — automatically and instantly.

Why it matters

The consequences of poor waste segregation reach far beyond the bin

Environmental protection

Correct segregation reduces landfill overflow, prevents soil and water contamination, and significantly lowers greenhouse gas emissions from decomposing mixed waste.

Resource recovery

Separating recyclables from organic waste ensures valuable materials like plastics and metals re-enter the production cycle instead of being permanently lost to landfill.

Cleaner institutions

Automated sorting removes dependency on manual effort and individual awareness, maintaining consistent hygiene and compliance standards in high-traffic facilities.

Data-driven management

Every classification is logged and monitored in real time, giving facility managers evidence-based insight into waste generation patterns and sorting performance.

How it works

Four automated steps — from deposit to sorted bin

1

Object detected

Ultrasonic sensor detects waste placed within 20cm and triggers the system automatically

2

Image captured

ESP32-CAM photographs the waste and transmits it to the Flask server via WiFi

3

AI classifies

MobileNetV2 model analyses the image and returns an organic or inorganic result

4

Bin opens

Servo motor rotates to direct the waste into the correct compartment automatically
